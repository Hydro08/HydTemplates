Quiz rules

  • You answer one question at a time.
  • Pick an answer and the Next button will appear. You can change your answer until you press Next.
  • You cannot go back to a previous question.
  • You can take each quiz only once.
  • Do not leave the tab. If you do, your quiz is cancelled and you must start again.
  • Copy, paste and right-click are disabled.
  • When the time is up, your quiz is submitted automatically.
